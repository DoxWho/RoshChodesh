How to Play

Your turn

Choose an empty square. A question comes up, and both teams work out an answer. Each captain writes the team’s answer on a board before time runs out.

Time’s up

Both captains hold up their boards. Then the answer is revealed.

Claim or steal

If the team whose turn it is has it right, they claim the square. If they miss and the other team has it right, the other team steals it.

To win

Three in a row.
